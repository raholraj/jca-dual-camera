# Dual Camera kaise use karein

## Important
Is app (Jetpack Camera App) me dual/concurrent camera **Settings** me hota hai.

## Steps

1. App open karo
2. **Settings icon** (gear) pe tap karo
3. Neeche scroll karo
4. **Concurrent Camera** option dhoondo
5. Use **ON / Dual** karo
6. Back button se camera pe aao
7. **Video mode** select karo (photo nahi)
8. Record button dabao

Ab front + back dono cameras record honge (PIP / side-by-side depending on device).

## Agar option nahi dikhta

- Aapka phone concurrent camera support nahi karta
- Settings me "Concurrent" search karke dekho
- Video mode me hi dual kaam karta hai (photo me nahi)

## Buttons bade kyun hain?

Jetpack Camera App ke buttons camera use ke liye intentionally bade touch targets hote hain.
Lag debug APK me normal hai; release build tez hoti hai.

## Naya APK build

Actions tab se **Build Dual Camera APK** run karke naya artifact download karo.
